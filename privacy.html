Privacy Policy

This policy explains how UVision handles information in the app, through Instagram in WebKit, and through support correspondence.

Effective date: November 12, 2026

1. About UVision

UVision is an iOS application developed by Valentin Lisogor, an individual developer. It opens Instagram in an embedded Apple WebKit WKWebView and applies local interface changes. UVision is not Instagram or Meta and does not provide a separate form for Instagram credentials. Instagram is a third-party service; UVision is not its operator.

2. Information handled through Instagram and WebKit

The app loads Instagram pages and resources in WKWebView. Those requests go to Instagram/Meta and other domains Instagram or Meta may select. As with web browsing, those services and network operators may receive technical and account-related information through requests; Instagram may use cookies and other website storage. Information entered on Instagram’s own pages, including login details and messages, is submitted through that service. Meta determines its own purposes, means, retention, and deletion. See the Meta Privacy Policy for Instagram. UVision does not control or make promises about Meta’s processing.

The app uses WebKit’s default persistent website data store. Instagram website data may remain on the device, including cookies, local website storage, and cached data. The reviewed native code does not read or export Instagram passwords, cookies, or access tokens.

Injected JavaScript reads page structure and limited interface text locally to identify routes and elements used by UVision features. For the native profile control, the page can send a route/profile status, profile path, and avatar image URL to the app. The reviewed code keeps these values in memory while running; it does not store them in UserDefaults. The native control can request the avatar image from the URL provided by Instagram. The reviewed message bridge does not send post or message text or login credentials to the native app. This is limited to reviewed UVision code and does not describe all processing by Instagram’s website.

3. Information stored by UVision on the device

The reviewed app stores the selected service identifier (instagram) in UserDefaults through SwiftUI @AppStorage, so it can reopen the selected service. This preference is stored locally. Route state, profile path, and avatar URL are held in app memory while the app runs. WebKit separately stores website data for pages opened in the web view.

UVision Settings asks for confirmation before clearing WebKit website data. It selects records whose displayed names are instagram.com or subdomains, or cdninstagram.com or subdomains, and asks WebKit to remove all website-data types for those records. It then clears the in-memory profile path/avatar and loads Instagram’s login page. The selected-service preference is retained. This scoped, best-effort cleanup may leave data for other domains Instagram uses and does not delete information held by Meta.

If no email app is available, UVision may offer to copy the support address to your clipboard; this copies only the address, not a message. The support mailbox is provided through the developer’s free personal Microsoft Outlook.com account. If you send an email, your email address, message, attachments, and any other information you choose to include are handled by the email app and provider you use and delivered to the support mailbox. The developer uses these messages to respond to users, investigate technical issues, address complaints and safety or security concerns, consider feedback and suggestions, and review or respond to collaboration proposals. Email addresses are not used for marketing mailings and are not sold. Correspondence is kept only for as long as necessary for these purposes and to meet applicable legal obligations. After a technical support request or bug report is resolved, it is moved to a separate “UVision Support” folder, which is periodically reviewed; messages are deleted when they are no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations. Feedback, thanks, suggestions, and collaboration proposals are considered separately and do not have to be moved to that folder; they are retained only while needed for consideration, response, ongoing collaboration, or legal obligations. Complaints and safety or security concerns are handled as needed to investigate and resolve them and meet legal obligations. A user may request deletion at uvision.help@outlook.com; requests are handled subject to applicable law and permitted exceptions. The reviewed app has no support-message backend or in-app message database. Microsoft processes mailbox data under its own service practices and terms; deletion by the developer does not guarantee immediate or final removal from Microsoft’s systems. See Microsoft’s Privacy Statement. Microsoft states that personal data may be stored and processed in your region, the United States, and other jurisdictions where Microsoft or its affiliates, subsidiaries, or service providers operate facilities, and describes safeguards for international transfers. UVision cannot determine the location or transfer mechanism applicable to a particular mailbox message.

UVision also links to its official Instagram profile. Contact with that profile is handled by Instagram/Meta under their terms and privacy practices, which UVision does not control.

5. Logs, diagnostics, analytics, and advertising

The reviewed source contains system event logs using event labels and limited values such as route categories, frame flags, allowlisted diagnostic labels/tags, and counts. Reviewed logging code does not intentionally include full URLs or query strings, passwords, cookies, account names, or post/message text. Some avatar-loading outcomes are logged as success/failure/empty events. Additional Home diagnostic modes are disabled by default. No code was found that uploads these logs to a UVision server.

The current UVision version is free and does not serve its own advertisements or include advertising or analytics SDKs, subscriptions, in-app payments, or a donation mechanism. Instagram/Meta may display advertising within its own service and processes data independently. No UVision-operated backend endpoint or integrated analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK was found in the reviewed iOS project. This does not describe or exclude network activity initiated by Instagram inside WebKit. iOS and Apple system diagnostics may handle system logs under device settings and Apple’s practices; the app does not set their retention period.

6. Camera, microphone, and photo library

The iOS configuration contains camera, microphone, and photo-library usage descriptions for features presented through Instagram in the web view. The reviewed native Swift code does not itself capture camera or microphone input, present a native photo picker, or call native photo-library APIs. Instagram may request system-controlled access when you use a relevant feature. iOS presents and enforces its permission controls. The reviewed project has no native save-to-Photos feature.

7. Retention and deletion

The selected-service preference remains in UserDefaults until it is changed or the app’s data is removed from the device. WebKit website data may remain on the device. UVision’s clear-data action targets only the matching Instagram and CDN records described above; data for other domains may remain, and the action may end the Instagram session. UVision cannot delete information held by Meta or determine Meta’s retention period. The app does not set a retention schedule for operating-system logs.

Technical support requests and bug reports are moved to the separate “UVision Support” folder after resolution. The folder is periodically reviewed, and messages are deleted when they are no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations. Feedback, thanks, suggestions, and collaboration proposals are handled separately and do not have to be moved to this folder; they are kept only while needed for consideration, response, ongoing collaboration, or legal obligations. Complaints and safety or security concerns are retained as needed to investigate and resolve them and meet legal obligations. There is no fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule. A user may request deletion at uvision.help@outlook.com; requests are handled subject to applicable law and permitted exceptions. Microsoft Outlook.com processes mailbox data under its own service practices and terms; deletion by the developer does not guarantee immediate or final removal from Microsoft’s systems.

For questions or requests about information handled by UVision, contact uvision.help@outlook.com. Please do not send passwords, cookies, or access tokens. 2. Информация, обрабатываемая через Instagram и WebKit

Приложение загружает страницы и ресурсы Instagram во WKWebView. Запросы направляются Instagram/Meta и другим доменам, которые могут выбирать Instagram или Meta. Как и при обычном просмотре сайтов, эти сервисы и сетевые операторы могут получать технические сведения и информацию об аккаунте через запросы; Instagram может использовать cookies и другие веб-хранилища. Данные, вводимые на собственных страницах Instagram, включая данные входа и сообщения, передаются через этот сервис. Meta самостоятельно определяет цели, способы, сроки хранения и удаления данных. См. Политику конфиденциальности Meta для Instagram. UVision не контролирует обработку Meta и не даёт обещаний относительно неё.

Приложение использует постоянное хранилище сайтов WebKit по умолчанию. Данные сайта Instagram могут оставаться на устройстве, включая cookies, локальное веб-хранилище и кэш. Проверенный нативный код не читает и не экспортирует пароли Instagram, cookies или токены доступа.

Внедрённый JavaScript локально читает структуру страницы и ограниченный текст интерфейса, чтобы определять маршруты и элементы функций UVision. Для нативного элемента профиля страница может передать приложению состояние маршрута/профиля, путь профиля и URL изображения аватара. Проверенный код хранит эти значения в памяти во время работы, но не сохраняет в UserDefaults. Нативный элемент может запросить изображение по URL, предоставленному Instagram. Проверенный канал сообщений не передаёт нативному приложению текст публикаций или сообщений и данные входа. Это описание относится к проверенному коду UVision и не охватывает всю обработку сайтом Instagram.

3. Информация, хранящаяся UVision на устройстве

Проверенное приложение сохраняет идентификатор выбранного сервиса (instagram) в UserDefaults через SwiftUI @AppStorage, чтобы при следующем запуске открыть выбранный сервис. Это локальная настройка. Состояние маршрута, путь профиля и URL аватара находятся в памяти приложения во время работы. WebKit отдельно хранит данные сайтов, открытых во встроенном браузере.

В настройках UVision перед очисткой данных сайтов WebKit запрашивается подтверждение. Приложение выбирает записи с отображаемыми именами instagram.com и его поддоменов либо cdninstagram.com и его поддоменов и просит WebKit удалить для них все типы данных сайтов. Затем приложение очищает путь профиля и URL аватара в памяти и открывает страницу входа Instagram. Предпочтение выбранного сервиса сохраняется. Очистка ограничена совпавшими записями и выполняется по принципу разумных усилий: данные на других доменах Instagram могут остаться; сведения, хранящиеся у Meta, не удаляются.
